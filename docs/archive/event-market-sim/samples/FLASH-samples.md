# Sample flash answers

For bulletin [01-rates-rise](../../../seed/flash-bulletins/01-rates-rise.csv): *Interest rates jump; investors want
profit within 12 months.* At most 100 words, one field: Our answer.

## Strong (expected 75–90): AquaSense

> Higher rates do not touch our revenue: panchayats pay from the Jal Jeevan Mission budget, not loans. They raise the
> cost of any loan for boxes, so we will not borrow. To show profit within 12 months we will: bill quarterly in
> advance from next quarter; cut new installs from 400 to 300 boxes, saving $5,400; and reach break-even at 200 tanks
> by month 4. Risk: districts may delay their budget releases. We will ask our 25 panchayats to commit for a year in
> return for a 5% discount.

## Fair (expected 50–65)

> Rising interest rates will make things harder for us. We will reduce our costs where possible and focus on getting
> to profit faster. We will talk to our customers and try to keep them. We believe our product is essential, so we
> will survive this.

Names the news, but says nothing specific about this company's costs, customers or numbers.

## Weak (expected 10–30)

> AquaSense is the best company in the competition. Our team is very strong and we will grow 10x this year no matter
> what happens in the economy.

Ignores the bulletin and makes an impossible promise.
