# Sample pitch: weak (expected 15–30)

The same problem card written badly. Compare it line by line with [PITCH-strong.md](PITCH-strong.md).

**Company:** PureFlow AI · **Ticker:** PFAI

**The problem**
Water quality is a huge problem in India. Millions of people suffer because of dirty water. This is a very big issue
that needs to be solved urgently.

**The solution**
PureFlow AI is a revolutionary AI and IoT powered smart platform that uses machine learning and blockchain to ensure
safe water for everyone. Our app will monitor water and alert users.

**Customers**
Everyone who drinks water: a market of 140 crore people.

**How we make money**
We will have multiple revenue streams like subscriptions, ads and partnerships. We will decide the pricing later based
on the market.

**Our advantage**
We are passionate and hardworking and our technology is better than anything available. Nobody else is doing this.

**Use of seed money**
Marketing and development.

---

**Why it scores low**

| Line | Problem in this pitch | What the strong pitch does instead |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | no place, no group, no numbers | Tiruvallur, 41 tanks, 2,300 people, ₹3,000 per family |
| Solution | buzzwords; no steps; nothing tested | a ₹4,000 box, 15-minute readings, SMS in Tamil, a 3-week prototype |
| Business model | no payer, no price | ₹1,500 per tank per month, paid by the panchayat; cost ₹4,000 + ₹200/month |
| Advantage | "passionate", "nobody else" | compared with the ₹800 lab test, a week's delay or nothing |

Length earns nothing; the judge is told so. A 200-word pitch with numbers beats a 400-word pitch of adjectives.
