# The Finance team: the money people

**You are a piggy bank with $500,000 in it.** Your job is to make the piggy bank bigger by the morning.

## The big idea

Imagine 50 lemonade stands. Each stand is cut into 100,000 tiny pieces, like a pizza cut into 100,000 slices.
Each slice is called a **share**. You can buy slices. If the stand becomes popular, each slice is worth more. If the
stand has a bad day, each slice is worth less.

**Buy slices that will get more valuable. Sell slices that will get less valuable.**

## Your friends (your squad)

At 21:00 the computer puts you in a team of three, called a **squad**:
- 🍋 one **Product** team (they run a lemonade stand),
- 🧑‍🏫 one **Consulting** team (they give advice),
- 💰 you.

You **must** buy 5,000 slices of your friend's stand at $10 each, right at the start. That gives them money to start,
and you keep those slices until the end. But **you can never buy or sell your friend's slices in the market**. That
would be cheating, because you know their secrets.

## How the night goes

1. **Read the stories (22:30).** Every stand writes a story about itself. Read them all. You can ask any stand a
   question on the Q&A board.
2. **The first sale (23:15).** Ask for slices of the stands you like: up to 4,000 slices of each. If too many people
   want the same stand, everyone gets a bit less.
3. **Buying and selling (from 23:30).** Every 15 minutes there is a **round**. You put in your orders: BUY or SELL.
   When the round ends, the price moves:
   - lots of people buying → the price goes **up** 📈
   - lots of people selling → the price goes **down** 📉
   - **everyone gets the same new price**, so if everyone buys the same stand, everyone pays more!
4. **Oh no! (00:30).** Something bad happens to every stand. All prices drop by 15%.
5. **Helping your friend (by 02:45).** Your friend's stand needs money. You give them $40,000 to $80,000 and get
   cheap slices back. All three of you must press **Sign**. If anyone changes anything, everyone signs again.
6. **Surprise news (04:00).** Something happens in the world. Prices move again.
7. **The end (05:00).** Everything stops. We count your money.

## Two magic rules

- **The robot teacher counts too.** Every stand gets marks from a robot teacher (the AI judge). The final price is
  **half** what the players think and **half** what the teacher thinks. A stand with bad marks can't stay expensive,
  however much people buy it.
- **Betting down (shorting).** If you think a stand will go down, you can "short" it: sell slices you borrowed. If the
  price goes down, you win. If it goes up, you lose. You must keep extra money locked to do this.

## Limits (so nobody breaks the game)

- At most **4,000** slices of any one stand.
- At most **2,000** borrowed (shorted) slices of any one stand.
- You need enough money in your piggy bank for every order.

## Helpers on the screen

- The **purple bar** at the top always says what to do next. Tap it.
- Price pages have **pictures (charts)** of each lemonade stand's price going up and down. Your **Positions** page draws your piggy bank growing.
- Talk to your squad in **Chat** on the site.

## How you win

At 05:00: **your money + your slices × the final price**. The team whose piggy bank grew the most wins. 🏆
There's also a prize for the fund that would have done best using only the robot teacher's prices.

## Don't

- Don't copy another fund's orders. The computer notices.
- Don't share your password.
