WhatsApp or Telegram broadcast to team captains. Times are IST; "auto" moments happen without a click (RUNBOOK.md).

## If you pause

> "We've paused the event because [reason]. The clock is frozen; when we resume you get back exactly the time you had.
> Nothing you saved is lost."

Resume only after telling the room: "Resuming in one minute."
