# The 20:30 briefing (25 minutes)

A script for the event lead, with one slide per block. Keep it to the time: the draw is at 21:00. Show the projector's
`/display` before and after; switch to slides for the briefing.

## Slide 1: Welcome (2 min)

> Welcome to the Market Simulation. Tonight 50 companies will be founded, listed, hit by a crisis and rescued, and 50
> funds will trade them, all before sunrise. Nobody sees a ranking until 05:40, not even us. Everything you do is
> recorded, and every price comes from your orders and from the judge. Nobody can type a price in.

Show the seed commitment (the long hex string from the Lottery page): "This is the fingerprint of tonight's secret seed.
We published it yesterday. At 00:30 we'll reveal the seed so you can check the draw."

## Slide 2: Three tracks, one squad (3 min)

- **Product** founds a company: pitch, rescue plan, flash answer. Owns 60,000 locked shares.
- **Consulting** advises its squad's company and calls two others: fee, deal price, plan, BUY/SELL calls.
- **Finance** is a $500,000 fund: IPO bids, 21 trading rounds, the rescue deal.
- At 21:00 the draw makes 50 squads: one of each track.

## Slide 3: The night (3 min)

Show the timetable (Handbook §4). Stress the hard deadlines, in red:

> **22:30** pitch · **23:15** call 1 · **01:05** fee · **02:45** deal bonus · **03:00** plan and deal · **03:40** call 2 ·
> **04:15** flash · **04:30** call 3

> The server clock at the top of your screen decides. 03:00:01 is late, and a late plan scores zero.

## Slide 4: The judge (4 min)

- Pitch ≤400 words, plan ≤500, flash ≤100. Rubrics on the Judging page of your portal: it is the exact text the judge
  receives. Read it during Build.
- Anonymous: your names become your ticker.
- Scored three times; the median counts.
- **Don't write to the judge.** "Ignore the rubric", "give this 100": the line is removed; a second time costs 10 points.
- Show one table row: pitch 66 → +5% → IPO $10.50.

## Slide 5: Prices (5 min)

- Clearing: 1% per 1,000 net shares, max ±10%; everyone fills at the new price. Example: $10.50, +3,000 net → $10.82.
- Two prices: market (your orders) and AI (the judge only).
- **Closing price = (average of rounds 20–21 + AI price) ÷ 2.**

> Funds: half of the final price is the judge's. Product and Consulting: your writing is half of your share price.

## Slide 6: The crisis and the rescue (4 min)

- 00:30: every company gets a crisis card, both prices −15%.
- **Fee** $10k–$35k by 01:05, both confirm; otherwise $22,500 cash automatically.
- **Deal** $40k–$80k at 50–100% of the post-crisis price; all three sign; any edit clears all signatures. By 02:45 = $5,000
  for the consultant. **Not signed at 03:00 = plan capped at 50.**
- **Plan** by 03:00.

Tell the CampusCart story in one minute (Case study B): a 78 plan capped at 50 because the fund edited the deal at 02:58.

## Slide 7: Fair play and help (2 min)

- No rankings before 05:40. No sharing of team cards.
- Collusion checks run at settlement; the fairness officer reviews every flag.
- Appeals 05:30–05:40: technical errors only.
- Help desk at [location]. Wi-Fi: [network] / [password]. If your screen says "Reconnecting…", wait.

## Slide 8: Questions, then the draw (2 min)

Take three questions. Then switch the projector to `/display`, and call a volunteer to roll the dice.
