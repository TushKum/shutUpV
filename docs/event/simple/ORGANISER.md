# The organisers: the grown-ups who run the game

**You are the referee.** You don't play. You press the buttons at the right time, keep everyone safe, and make
sure it's fair.

## The big idea

The computer does most of the work by itself: it opens and closes rounds, moves prices and counts the money. You do the
few things a computer shouldn't do alone: start the night, roll the dice, let the robot teacher mark the work, and
open the awards.

**Nobody can change a price by hand. Not even you.** That's what makes it fair.

## Who does what

| Person | Job |
|---|---|
| 👑 Event lead | Runs the night on the **Phase** page, talks to the room |
| 🏦 Exchange desk | Watches the rounds, fixes money mistakes (needs a second person to approve) |
| 🤖 AI desk | Starts the robot teacher (AI judge) and releases the marks |
| ⚖️ Fairness officer | Checks the "is someone cheating?" flags at the end |
| 💻 Tech helper | Watches the **Health** page and the Wi-Fi |
| 🎟️ Registration desk | Hands out login cards |

## The night, step by step

| Time | What you do |
|---|---|
| 20:00 | Hand out cards. Click **Advance** to start the night. |
| 20:30 | Explain the game (use the briefing script). |
| 21:00 | **The draw.** Type the secret seed and roll the dice. The computer makes the squads. |
| 22:30 | Stories (pitches) close by themselves. AI desk clicks **Start judging (Pitch)**. |
| 23:15 | Click **Seal missing as 0**, then **Release pitch scores**. Prices appear. |
| 23:30 | Trading starts by itself. Just watch. |
| 00:30 | **Oh no!** The crisis happens by itself. Tell the room. |
| 03:00 | Plans close. AI desk clicks **Start judging (Plan)** right away. |
| 03:30 | **Seal missing as 0**, **Release plan scores**. |
| 04:00 | Click **Publish the flash bulletin** (the surprise news). |
| 04:15 | Click **Start judging (Flash)** quickly. You only have 5 minutes! |
| 04:20 | **Seal missing as 0**, **Release flash scores**. |
| 05:00 | The market closes by itself. Ring the bell! 🔔 |
| 05:00–05:40 | The fairness officer checks the flags. |
| 05:40 | Click **Advance** to the awards. The winners appear on the big screen. 🏆 |

## When something goes wrong

**Rule one: press Pause first, think second.** Pause freezes the clock for everyone. When you press Resume, everyone
gets exactly the time they had left.

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Teams can't log in | Try phone data instead of Wi-Fi. Lost card? Reprint it at the desk. |
| Screens say "Reconnecting…" | Wait. They fix themselves in a few seconds. |
| Rounds don't move | Keep the Phase page open (it helps the clock tick). Use **Close round now** if needed. |
| The robot teacher is slow | Open another laptop with "Run the judge here". |
| The robot teacher is broken | Switch to the backup model. If both are broken, use the human judges (HUMAN-JUDGING.md). |
| Someone's money is wrong | Ledger → Corrections. One person asks, a second person approves. |
| The room needs more time | Phase → **Extend**. |

## Golden rules

- 🙈 **No rankings before 05:40.** Not on the big screen, not in your words.
- 🔐 **Keep the secret seed and the cards file secret.** Never post them anywhere.
- ⏰ **The computer's clock is the boss.** 03:00:01 is late.
- 🤝 **Tell the room before you resume** after a pause.
