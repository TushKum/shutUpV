# The Consulting team: the wise helpers

**You are the helper who gives good advice.** You help your friend's lemonade stand, and you guess what will happen to
two other stands.

## The big idea

There are 50 lemonade stands. Each one is cut into 100,000 slices called **shares**. Slices go up in value when a stand
does well, and down when it does badly. You **can't buy or sell slices**. You earn money by **helping** and by
**guessing right**.

## Your friends (your squad)

At 21:00 the computer puts you in a team of three, called a **squad**:
- 🍋 one **Product** team (the lemonade stand you help),
- 🧑‍🏫 you,
- 💰 one **Finance** team (the investors).

You also get **2 other stands to watch**, from other squads.

## How the night goes

1. **Help with the story (21:10–22:30).** Your stand writes its pitch. Help them make it great.
2. **Guess #1 (by 23:15).** For each of your 2 stands, say **BUY** (it'll go up) or **SELL** (it'll go down).
3. **Oh no! (00:30).** Something bad happens to every stand. Now your stand really needs you.
4. **Your pay (by 01:05).** You and your stand agree what you get paid: $10,000 to $35,000, in money or slices. You
   **both** press confirm. If you don't, you automatically get $22,500.
5. **The rescue deal (by 02:45).** You choose the **price** for the deal: how much the investors pay per slice
   (between half and all of the after-crisis price). The investors choose **how much money**. All three press **Sign**.
   🎁 **Signed by 02:45 = a $5,000 bonus for you.**
6. **The rescue plan (by 03:00).** Write it together with your stand: up to 500 words. **Late = 0 marks.** No signed deal =
   at most 50 marks.
7. **Guess #2 (03:30–03:40)** and **Guess #3 (04:20–04:30).** BUY or SELL again for your 2 stands.
8. **Surprise news (04:00).** Help your stand write a short answer (100 words).
9. **The end (05:00).** We count.

## How you earn

| What | How much |
|---|---|
| Your pay from the stand | $10,000 – $35,000 |
| Rescue plan marks | $500 for every mark above 50 (and you **lose** $500 for every mark below 50) |
| Deal signed by 02:45 | $5,000 |
| Each right guess | $2,500 (6 guesses) |

A guess is right only if the price really moved your way. If it stays exactly the same, it counts as wrong.

## How you win

At 05:00: **your money + any slices you were paid × the final price.** Most money wins. 🏆

## Tips

- The plan marks are your biggest prize: 90 marks = +$20,000!
- Get the deal signed early. Late deals hurt everyone.
- Being too greedy (a huge fee, or a super-cheap deal with a weak plan) makes the fairness officer look closely.
- The **purple bar** at the top always says what to do next. Tap it.
- Talk to your squad in **Chat** on the site. Agree the fee there, then do it on the Rescue page.
