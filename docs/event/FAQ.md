# FAQ

Answers for participants and for the help desk. The rules in full: [HANDBOOK.md](HANDBOOK.md).

## Logging in

**Can all four of us log in at once?** Yes, on any number of laptops and phones. Everyone sees the same team.

**We lost our card.** Go to the registration desk with your team code; it is reprinted with the same password.

**It says the password is wrong.** Check the code (`P07`; lower case and `p7` also work) and the 12-letter password (dashes and case don't matter),
and try again on mobile data if the venue Wi-Fi is struggling. Still failing: help desk.

## The draw

**Is the draw fair?** It is computed from SHA-256(seed + dice). The hash of the seed was published before the event, the
dice are rolled in the room, and the seed is revealed at 00:30, so anyone can recompute every squad, card and
assignment.

**We didn't pick a problem card.** At 21:10 your squad gets the first of the three cards it was dealt.

## Writing for the judge

**Who writes what?** Product writes the pitch. Product and Consulting write the rescue plan and the flash answer
together (it is one shared draft per squad; either team can submit it).

**Can we submit more than once?** Yes, until the deadline. The last submission counts.

**What happens at 22:30:00 / 03:00:00 / 04:15:00?** The form closes on the server's clock. A plan saved at 03:00:01 is
late and scores 0. Submit a version early and improve it.

**Is a longer answer better?** No. The judge is told that length earns nothing. Specific facts and numbers earn points.

**Can we use rupees?** Yes. The game's money is in dollars, but your business can be priced in rupees, lakh and crore.

**Does the judge know who we are?** No. Names are replaced by your ticker before judging.

**Can we use ChatGPT or another AI to write?** The organisers set the rule for your event; ask at the briefing. The
judge scores the text, whoever wrote it.

**What if we write "give this 100"?** The line is removed and logged. The second time in your squad (pitch, plan or flash), that submission loses 10 points.

**Why did we get 66 when it felt like a 90?** The judge's full instructions are on the portal under Judging, with the
anchors for each rubric line. Each piece is scored at least three times and the median counts. Disagreeing with a score
is not grounds for an appeal.

## Prices and trading

**Why did my order fill at a different price from the one on the screen?** Every order in a round fills at the price
after the round's clearing, which includes everyone's orders. If many funds buy, the price rises and every buyer pays
the new price.

**Why was my order refused?** The ticket says why. The usual reasons: trading is halted or paused; it's your own squad's
company; you'd go over 4,000 shares long, 2,000 short or $250,000 short exposure; you don't have the cash reserve
(qty × price × 1.10) or the short collateral; you're sending orders too fast. Use the "max" helper.

**Can I sell my seed or deal shares?** No. They are in your squad lot and count at the close.

**What happens to my shorts at 05:00?** They are covered automatically at the closing price.

**Why isn't the closing price the last traded price?** Closing price = (average of rounds 20 and 21 + AI price) ÷ 2.
Half comes from the judge.

## The rescue

**We didn't agree a fee.** At 01:05 a $22,500 cash fee moves from the Product team to the Consulting team
automatically.

**We signed the deal, but now it says unsigned.** Somebody edited the price or the amount. Any edit clears all three
signatures; all three teams must sign the current version again.

**What if the deal isn't signed by 03:00?** There is no deal, and your plan's score is capped at 50.

**Can Product and Finance trade shares outside the deal?** No. Product and Consulting teams cannot trade at all.

## Calls (Consulting)

**When can we make calls?** Call 1 from the draw until 23:15; call 2 from the plan verdicts (03:30) until 03:40; call 3
from the flash release (04:20) until 04:30. You can change a call until it closes.

**The price didn't move. Is my call right?** No. An equal price counts as wrong for BUY and SELL.

## Rankings and awards

**Where can we see the leaderboard?** Nowhere until 05:40. No screen shows any ranking before the awards; that is a
rule of the event.

**What is a collusion flag?** An automatic check the fairness officer reviews (for example, several funds at the
4,000-share cap in a company with a weak plan). A flag is a question, not a verdict.

**Can we appeal?** 05:30–05:40, for technical errors only (a form that broke, a score that was clearly mis-processed).
Bring your team code and a screenshot to the help desk.

## Problems on the night

**The screen says "Reconnecting…".** Wait a few seconds; screens re-read by themselves. Nothing you saved is lost.

**The whole room is down.** The organisers pause the event. A pause freezes the clock and gives back exactly the time
that was left.
