# Demo video

[market-simulation-demo.mp4](market-simulation-demo.mp4) (5 min 20 s, 6 MB): a full practice night with 150 teams
at 20× speed. One Product, one Consulting and one Finance team are played through their real screens; bots play
the other 147 teams and the organisers' buttons. The projector screen is shown too.

| Part | What you see |
|---|---|
| 1. Sign in and the squad draw | Signing in with a team card; the draw on the projector with its seed check |
| 2. Product: the pitch | Picking a problem card; typing and submitting the pitch |
| 3. Consulting: calls | BUY/SELL calls on the two covered companies |
| 4. Finance: IPO and trading | The pitch book, IPO prices, IPO bids, the order ticket, prices moving round by round |
| 5. Crisis and rescue | The crisis screen (−15%), the fee proposed and confirmed, the deal priced, funded and signed by all three |
| 6. Plan and verdicts | The rescue plan submitted; the verdicts screen with top risers and fallers |
| 7. Flash news | The bulletin on the projector; the Product team's answer |
| 8. Close and awards | The closing bell; the awards |

Two things to say when you show it:

- **The scores are random.** The rehearsal used the practice judge (`JUDGE_FAKE=1`), so the scores are not a real
  assessment. The demo company's plan scored 26 and fell 20%; on the night the real AI judge scores the writing.
- **The fund's order was refused** because it already held the 4,000-share maximum in that company. The message
  is visible on screen; the IPO bids and the price board show trading.
