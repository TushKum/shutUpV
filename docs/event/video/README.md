# Demo video

[market-simulation-demo.mp4](market-simulation-demo.mp4) (5 min 15 s, 7 MB): a full practice night with 150 teams
at 20× speed. One squad (Product XP50, Consulting XC17, Finance XF02) is played through its real screens; bots play
the other 147 teams and the organisers' buttons. The projector and the fairness officer's control panel are shown
too.

| Part | What you see |
|---|---|
| 1. Sign in, the draw and the squad chat | Signing in; the draw on the projector with its seed check; picking a problem card; the squad saying hello in its chat (it arrives live on the fund's screen); the "Do this now" bar |
| 2. Product: the pitch | Typing and submitting the pitch |
| 3. Consulting: calls | BUY/SELL calls on the two covered companies |
| 4. Finance: IPO, trading and charts | The pitch book, IPO prices, IPO bids; the order ticket with a quick amount and its one-sentence summary; prices moving with the news ticker underneath; trend lines, a company's price chart and the fund's portfolio chart |
| 5. Crisis, fee and rescue deal | The crisis screen (−15%); the fee named and agreed in the squad chat, then proposed and confirmed by both teams; the deal priced, funded and signed by all three; the fairness officer reading and searching the squad chats |
| 6. Plan and verdicts | The rescue plan submitted; the verdicts screen with top risers and fallers; an alert that the scores are out |
| 7. Flash news | The bulletin on the projector; the Product team's answer |
| 8. Close and awards | The closing bell; the awards; the fund's final result |

When you show it, say that **the scores are random**: the rehearsal used the practice judge (`JUDGE_FAKE=1`), so the
scores are not a real assessment. On the night the real AI judge scores the writing.
