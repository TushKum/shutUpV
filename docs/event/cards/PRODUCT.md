# Product team card (P01–P50)

**You are the company.** You hold 60,000 locked shares. Your final value = **retained shares × closing price + cash.**
Tie-break: higher plan score. You can never trade.

| When | Do | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 21:00–21:10 | **Pick 1 of your 3 problem cards** | No pick → the first card dealt. |
| 21:10–22:30 | **Write and submit the pitch** (Pitch page) | ≤400 words. Company name + 3–4 letter ticker. Hard close **22:30**. Resubmit freely before then. |
| 22:30–23:30 | **Answer fund questions** (Q&A) | ≤100 words each, all public. Funds read them before the IPO. |
| 23:15 | Your IPO price | $10.00 × (1 + pitch tier): $9.00–$11.00. |
| 00:30 | **Crisis card** | Both prices −15% = your post-crisis price. |
| 00:30–01:05 | **Agree the fee** with your consultant | $10,000–$35,000, cash and/or ≤3,000 shares at the IPO price. Both confirm. Else $22,500 cash at 01:05. |
| by 02:45 | **Sign the deal** | Fund invests $40k–$80k at 50–100% of post-crisis price. Your shares go to the fund. Any edit clears all signatures. |
| by 03:00 | **Rescue plan** with your consultant | ≤500 words. **Late = 0. No signed deal at 03:00 = capped at 50.** |
| 04:00–04:15 | **Flash answer** | ≤100 words on the bulletin. |
| 05:40 | Awards | |

**How your money moves**

- Seed: +$50,000 (5,000 shares to your fund at $10.00).
- Fee: −cash and/or −shares to the consultant.
- Deal: +amount in cash, −shares (amount ÷ price) to your fund.
- IPO money goes to the exchange, not to you.

**Think about it this way**

- Every share you give away is a share you don't own at the close. Every dollar you take in stays as cash.
- A cheap deal price means more shares out for the same money. A higher price keeps more shares but the fund may
  walk away; without a signed deal your plan is capped at 50.
- The closing price is half market, half AI price. Strong writing (plan +25%, flash +5%) is the only lever you
  control directly.

**Pitch sections**: The problem · The solution · Customers · How we make money · Our advantage · Use of seed money.
**Plan sections**: The crisis · The new plan · The money · The rescue deal · Time to recovery · Risks · Next three steps.
**Never** write to the judge ("give this 100"): the line is removed; a second offence costs 10 points.
