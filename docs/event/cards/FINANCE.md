# Finance team card (F01–F50)

**You are an investment fund with $500,000.** Final value = **cash + long shares × closing price − short shares ×
closing price**, ranked by return on $500,000. Tie-break: smaller largest single position.

| When | Do | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 21:00 | Squad formed: you buy **5,000 seed shares** of your squad's company at $10.00 (−$50,000) | Squad lot: not tradable, not counted in your limits. |
| 22:30–23:15 | **Read the pitch book; ask questions** on the Q&A board | Answers ≤100 words, all public. |
| 23:15–23:30 | **IPO bids**: 0–4,000 shares per company | Not your squad's company. Total ≤ your cash at IPO prices. Oversubscribed: request × 35,000 ÷ total, rounded down to 10. |
| 23:30–00:30 | Rounds 1–4 (15 min) | |
| 00:30 | Crisis: trading halted, all prices −15% | |
| 00:45–03:00 | Rounds 5–12 (15 min; break 01:45–02:00) | |
| by 02:45 | **Rescue deal**: you set the amount, $40,000–$80,000 | Price set by the consultant (50–100% of post-crisis). All three sign; any edit clears all signatures. |
| 03:30–05:00 | Rounds 13–21 (10 min). Flash bulletin 04:00. | |
| 05:00 | Close: all shorts covered at the closing price | |

**Orders**: BUY · SELL (shares you own) · SHORT · COVER. Edit or cancel until the round closes.

**Clearing**: net = (BUY + COVER) − (SELL + SHORT). Price moves **1% per 1,000 net shares, max ±10%**. **Every order
fills at the new price.** E.g. $10.50, buys 2,000 + 1,500, sell 500 → +3% → all fill at $10.82.

**Limits**

| Limit | Rule |
|---|---|
| Long | ≤4,000 shares per company from the exchange (IPO included) |
| Short | ≤2,000 shares per company; ≤$250,000 total short exposure |
| Buy reserve | qty × price × 1.10 held when you enter the order |
| Short collateral | 150% of short value (× 1.10 on entry), recomputed every round; short of it = new buys/shorts blocked |
| Insider | never your own squad's company |

**Think about it this way**

- The closing price is **half market, half AI price**. The AI price moves only with the judge's plan and flash
  scores. A company with a weak plan has a low anchor, however hard the market bids it up.
- Everyone buying the same stock in the same round pushes up everyone's fill price.
- Best-judging fund award: your portfolio valued at AI prices only.
- Three or more funds at the 4,000 cap in a company whose plan scores below 50 raises a collusion flag; so do two funds
  with near-identical orders.
