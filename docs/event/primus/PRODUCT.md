# PRIMUS for Product teams, explained simply

**You run a startup.** Your company has shares, like a pizza cut into 100,000 slices. You keep 60,000. The better
your company does, the more each slice is worth. That is your score: **your slices × the final price + your money.**

## Your sector

At 21:00 the lottery puts you in a squad (you, a consulting firm and an investment fund) and gives your squad a
**sector**: Luxury, FMCG or Energy. Everything you do tonight comes from that sector. You'll see it as a coloured
badge at the top of every page.

## The three rounds

1. **Round 1 · Build (21:10–22:00).** Write your **PRD**: your startup's plan, in seven short parts, up to 800
   words. A robot judge marks it out of 100. Good marks = a higher starting price. Then answer investors' questions.
2. **Round 2 · Four crises (22:45–04:45).** Four times, a **crisis card** arrives: something bad happens to your
   company and every price falls 5%. Each time:
   - **hire a consulting firm** in the first 20 minutes (offer them money or slices; they can say yes, no or ask
     for more);
   - **write your response plan** (up to 500 words) before the due time, using your firm's advice;
   - **rate your firm** 1 to 5 stars.
   A good plan pushes your price up; a weak or missing one pushes it down.
3. **Round 3 · The final problem (04:45–06:30).** In the **draft** you pick a problem statement from your sector,
   lowest score so far first, 20 seconds each. Then hire a firm and write your **solution** together. Companies on
   the same statement compete: the best gets the biggest price rise.

## Tips

- Your Home page says **"Do this now"** with a countdown. Do that.
- The step row at the top shows where you are in each crisis.
- When a crisis arrives your card shows up big on screen. Read it carefully: your plan must answer it.
