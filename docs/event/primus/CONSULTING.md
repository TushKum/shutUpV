# PRIMUS for Consulting firms, explained simply

**You are a firm of advisers.** Companies pay you to help them through their crises. Your score is the money you
earn: **fees + bonuses + the value of any shares you were paid in.**

## How you earn

1. **Round 1 · Prove yourself (21:10–22:00).** Every firm writes a memo on the same case. A good memo earns a
   bonus and builds your name.
2. **Round 2 · Win clients (22:45–04:45).** In each of the four crises:
   - companies send you **offers**: money, shares or both. Accept, decline or ask for more. You can take **two
     clients** per crisis;
   - write an **advice memo** for each client before the due time. **No memo = no fee**;
   - your clients **rate you** 1 to 5 stars. Good memos and good stars earn a bonus; poor ones cost you.
3. **Round 3 · The last job (04:45–06:30).** Get hired again and help your client write its solution.

## Tips

- Fill in your **profile** early: companies choose a firm from it.
- Your Home page lists every offer to answer and every memo to write, with countdowns.
- Read each client's crisis card and plan, and give advice with numbers that fit them.
