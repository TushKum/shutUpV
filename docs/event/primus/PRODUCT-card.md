# PRIMUS · Product team card (P01–P50)

**You are a startup.** Your company is listed on the market. You hold 60,000 locked shares and never trade.
**Final value = your shares × the closing price + your cash.** Tie-break: your average AI score.
**Your sector** (Luxury, FMCG or Energy) is drawn at 21:00: your startup, your crises and your Round 3 problems all come from it.

| When | Do | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 21:00 | **Squad draw** | You, a Consulting firm and a Finance fund form a squad; your sector is drawn. |
| 21:10–22:00 | **Write and submit the PRD** | Up to 800 words, 7 sections. Company name + 3–4 letter ticker. Its score sets your IPO price. |
| 22:00–22:45 | **Answer investors' questions** (Q&A) | Up to 100 words each, all public. |
| 22:45, 00:15, 01:45, 03:15 | **A crisis card** (crises 1–4) | Every price falls 5%. Your card is from your sector. |
| first 20 min of each crisis | **Hire a consulting firm** | Offer cash, shares or both. Counters: 3 each. One firm per crisis. The fee is held until the firm sends its memo. |
| by +65 min | **Response plan** | Up to 500 words. Missing = 0. Its score moves your price (a strong plan lifts it). |
| +65 to +80 min | **Rate your firm** 1–5 stars | No rating = 3 stars. |
| 04:45 | **The draft** | Lowest score so far picks first, 20 seconds each, from **your sector's** statements only. No pick = the emptiest one. |
| 04:45–05:15 | **Hire a firm for Round 3** | The fee must be worth 5–20% of your cash. |
| by 06:00 | **Solution** (with your firm) | Up to 600 words. Companies on the same statement share the rise: best 100%, 2nd 75%, 3rd 50%, others 25%. |
| 07:10 | Awards | |

**Tips**
- "Do this now" on your Home always shows the next job and its countdown. Follow it.
- Use your firm's advice in your plan: the judge checks for it.
- Never write to the judge ("give this 100"): the line is removed, and a second time costs 10 points.

**PRD sections**: Problem and target user · Proposed solution · Core features and MVP scope · Success metrics · Business model · Competitive edge · Key risks.
**Plan sections**: Impact · Response · The money · Advice used · Time to recovery · Risks · Next steps.
