# PRIMUS · Consulting firm card (C01–C50)

**You are a consulting firm.** Companies hire you for advice and pay you fees.
**Final value = cash (fees and bonuses) + fee shares × the closing price.** Tie-break: your average client rating.

| When | Do | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 21:10–22:00 | **Crisis memo** (Round 1) | The same case for every firm, up to 500 words, 6 sections. Bonus: $500 × (score − 50). |
| 22:00–22:30 | **Call 1**: BUY or SELL on the companies you follow | A correct call earns money. |
| any time | **Fill in your firm's profile** | Headline, specialities, rate card, asking fee. Companies choose from it. |
| first 20 min of each crisis | **Answer offers** | Accept, decline or counter (3 counters each). At most 2 clients per crisis. Accepting is final. |
| by +65 min | **Advice memo for each client** | Up to 500 words. **No memo = no fee** (it goes back to the client). |
| after the scores | **Bonus** | $500 × (memo score − 50) + $1,000 × (stars − 3), for each client. |
| 05:00–06:00 | **Round 3** | Get hired, write your memo and help your client's solution (you can edit it with them). |
| 07:10 | Awards | |

**Tips**
- A complete profile wins more offers. Ratings are public and stay on your profile.
- Write each memo for that client's own crisis card and numbers; read their plan as it grows.
- Use the hire chat to agree with your client what they need.

**Memo sections**: Diagnosis · Options · Recommendation · Numbers · Risks · Next steps.
**Advice sections**: Diagnosis · What to do · Evidence and numbers · Why it fits your client.
