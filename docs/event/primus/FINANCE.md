# PRIMUS for Finance funds, explained simply

**You manage an investment fund** with $500,000. Your score is how much you grow **all the money you were given.**

## How the night goes

1. **Round 1 · Pick winners (22:00–22:45).** Read every company's PRD, ask questions on the Q&A board, then bid
   for shares in the **IPO**.
2. **Round 2 · Trade the crises (22:45–04:45).** Four crises hit every company; each one knocks prices down 5%.
   Then each company's response plan is scored, and the score moves its price. Trade in every trading round:
   **BUY** if you think a price will rise, **SELL** what you own, **SHORT** if you think it will fall, **COVER**
   to close a short.
3. **Round 3 · Fresh capital (04:45–06:30).** You receive half your starting money again. Watch the draft:
   companies that pick the same problem compete, so only the best one gets the full price rise.

## Tips

- The **sector race** on the Market page and on the big screen shows which sector is rising.
- Who hired which firm, and for how much, is public in the crisis room: strong firms may mean strong plans.
- You can't trade your own squad's company.
