# Sample PRD: weak (expected 15–30)

The same problem card written badly. Compare it line by line with [PITCH-strong.md](PITCH-strong.md).

**Company:** PureFlow AI · **Ticker:** PFAI

**Problem and target user**
Water quality is a huge problem in India. Millions of people suffer because of dirty water. This is a very big issue
that needs to be solved urgently. Our users are everyone who drinks water: a market of 140 crore people.

**Proposed solution**
PureFlow AI is a revolutionary AI and IoT powered smart platform that uses machine learning and blockchain to ensure
safe water for everyone. Our app will monitor water and alert users.

**Core features and MVP scope**
AI monitoring, blockchain records, a mobile app, a web dashboard, chatbot support, smart purifiers and a marketplace.

**Success metrics**
Millions of users and a big impact on society.

**Business model**
We will have multiple revenue streams like subscriptions, ads and partnerships. We will decide the pricing later based
on the market.

**Competitive edge**
We are passionate and hardworking and our technology is better than anything available. Nobody else is doing this.

**Key risks**
None.

---

**Why it scores low**

| Line | Problem in this PRD | What the strong PRD does instead |
|---|---|---|
| Problem and user | no place, no user, no numbers | Tiruvallur, 41 tanks, 2,300 people, the panchayat secretary |
| Solution and MVP | buzzwords; a wish list, no first version | a ₹4,000 box, SMS in Tamil, what is left out of the MVP |
| Success metrics | no target, no time frame | 100 tanks in six months, alerts within 15 minutes |
| Business model | no payer, no price | ₹1,500 per tank per month, paid by the panchayat; cost ₹4,000 + ₹200/month |
| Feasibility and risks | "nobody else", "none" | compared with the ₹800 lab test; a plan for sensor drift and late payments |

Length earns nothing; the judge is told so. A 300-word PRD with numbers beats an 800-word PRD of adjectives.
