# Sample rescue plan: strong (expected 80–92)

AquaSense after the **Supply shortage** crisis ("A key input disappears": the chip is unavailable for 18 months; stock
lasts six weeks). Deal: $60,000 at $7.50, 8,000 shares, signed. Scored 86, 88, 91 in the
[case study](../case-studies/AQUASENSE.md).

**The crisis**
Our chlorine sensor chip will be unavailable for 18 months. We have stock for 60 boxes, six weeks of installs. Without a
new chip we stop growing in six weeks and cannot replace failed boxes for our 100 paying tanks.

**The new plan**
1. Redesign the box around a second chip (a Bengaluru supplier, in stock, ₹300 more per box). Our engineer has tested a
   sample on the bench; the redesign takes three weeks.
2. Keep the 60 boxes in stock for repairs to paying tanks, so no customer loses alerts.
3. Pause new panchayats for four weeks, then restart installs with the new box.

**The money**
Cash now $30,000 after the fee. Monthly costs $3,000 (technicians, SMS, server). Revenue from 100 tanks: ₹1,50,000 a
month, about $1,800. Monthly burn before the deal: $1,200, so 25 months of cash, but no growth. The redesign costs
$6,000; 400 new boxes at $54 cost $21,600.

**The rescue deal**
Delta invests $60,000 at $7.50, 78% of the post-crisis price, for 8,000 shares (8% of the company). Cash after the deal:
$90,000. It pays for the redesign and 400 boxes and leaves $62,400 of runway, more than four years at our current burn.

**Time to recovery**
Week 3: new box tested in 5 tanks. Week 6: installs restart. Month 4: 200 tanks, revenue $3,600 a month, break-even.
Month 9: 400 tanks.

**Risks**
- The new chip may read chlorine differently. We will run old and new boxes side by side in 5 tanks for two weeks
  before switching.
- The ₹300 extra cost cuts our margin by 2%. We keep the price at ₹1,500.
- One supplier again. We are qualifying a third chip for the next batch.

**Next three steps**
1. Tomorrow: order 20 sample chips (₹6,000) and start the redesign.
2. This week: tell our 25 panchayats the plan and that service continues.
3. Week 3: side-by-side test in 5 tanks; publish the results to customers.
