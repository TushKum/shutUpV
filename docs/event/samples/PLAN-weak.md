# Sample rescue plan: weak (expected 35–49)

SnapStudy after the **Data breach** crisis ("Customer data is leaked"), from
[case study C](../case-studies/SNAPSTUDY.md), median 45.

**The crisis**
Some data was leaked but it was not really our fault because the drive was shared by a tutor. We think customers will
forget about it soon.

**The new plan**
We will implement military-grade security and make our platform the safest in the world. We will also launch a big
marketing campaign to rebuild our brand and get even more users than before.

**The money**
The money from the deal will be used for marketing and security. We are confident revenue will grow quickly.

**The rescue deal**
Our fund is investing in us because they believe in our vision.

**Time to recovery**
We will recover very soon, probably within a few weeks.

**Risks**
There are no major risks because our product is loved by students.

**Next three steps**
Fix security, do marketing, grow.

---

**Why it scores low**

| Rubric line | What is missing |
|---|---|
| Solves the crisis (25) | It blames a tutor, says customers will forget, and never tells the 3,000 students what was leaked or what they should do. |
| Money logic (20) | No cash figure, no costs, no runway. |
| Funding and deal (20) | No amount, price or shares, while the judge sees the deal terms from the game's records. |
| Time to recovery (15) | "A few weeks", with no milestones. |
| New risks (10) | "No major risks" after a data breach. |
| Next steps (10) | Three slogans, not steps. |

A better plan: tell every affected family within 24 hours what leaked; delete the shared drive and move photos to
storage with access logs (₹4,000 a month); give three months free; report to the college; show the cash with the deal
money spent on this first, not on marketing.
