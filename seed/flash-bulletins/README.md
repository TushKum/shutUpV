# Flash bulletins

One bulletin is published to every company at 04:00 (BRIEF: Flash news). Each file here is ready to upload on
**/admin/&lt;slug&gt;/content → Flash bulletin** (columns `title,body`, a header row and exactly one row). Pick one before
the night, upload it, check it on the Bulletins page, and keep the choice secret: the flash bulletin locks when it is
published at 04:00.

| File | News | Why it is a good flash |
|---|---|---|
| 01-rates-rise.csv | Interest rates jump; investors want profit in 12 months | **Recommended.** Hits every company (costs, customers, funding) without favouring a sector. |
| 02-fuel-shock.csv | Fuel up 30% | Hard on delivery and transport ideas, mild for software; a good test of "responds to the news". |
| 03-data-law.csv | A strict data rule in 30 days | Hard on apps that collect data; makes teams think about consent and cost. |
| 04-free-competitor.csv | A free government app | Every company must answer "why pay us". |
| 05-payment-fee.csv | 1.5% on every digital payment | Small numbers to reason with; tests pricing answers. |
| 06-heatwave.csv | Heatwave and power cuts | Operations and demand shift for six weeks; physical businesses suffer most. |

Write your own in the same format if you prefer. A good bulletin affects every company, can be answered in 100 words,
and names numbers and dates teams can use. Avoid news that obviously favours one sector of the problem deck (Water,
Transport, Health, Education, Food, Finance, Housing, Retail, Energy, Community).
